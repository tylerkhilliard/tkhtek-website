Security & Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Published by TKH Tek (tkhtek.com)

🛡️ The Zero Data Collection Pledge

ChronoLens is designed with privacy as its primary architecture. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any user data, photos, camera feeds, or personal identifiers. All image analysis and color calculations occur 100% on your device.

1. Overview

TKH Tek ("we", "us", or "our") develops assistive accessibility software for Apple platforms. This Privacy Policy applies to the ChronoLens mobile application and explains our handling of information and permissions.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Visual Imagery: Live camera frames and photos loaded from your library exist only in volatile device RAM while you are inspecting them. No images are saved remotely or sent over the internet.
  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, email address, physical location, or device hardware IDs.
  • Usage Telemetry & Analytics: ChronoLens does not contain any third-party analytics frameworks (e.g. Firebase, Mixpanel, Segment) or advertising networks.

3. System Permissions

  • Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription): Used exclusively to capture real-world objects for color inspection. The feed never leaves your iPhone.
  • Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription): Used via Apple PhotoKit to let you choose an existing image to analyze. Only the specific picture you select is temporarily read into memory.
  • Speech & Haptics: Utilizes iOS system AVFoundation and CoreHaptics to announce colors and pulse tactile rhythms. No audio is recorded.

4. Financial Security & In-App Purchases

Voluntary tips supporting independent development are handled exclusively through Apple's StoreKit 2 framework. All financial transactions are managed and secured by Apple. TKH Tek never receives, sees, or stores your credit card details or billing addresses.

5. App Store Privacy Certification

In accordance with Apple App Store guidelines, ChronoLens is certified with the "Data Not Collected" Privacy Nutrition Label.
