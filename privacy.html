Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Overview

TKH Tek builds privacy-first software. Our core design principle is simple: your data belongs to you. We believe the safest data is data that is never collected in the first place.

2. Data Collection & Camera Access

For applications that require visual input (such as ChronoLens):

  • Camera Frames: The camera feed is used strictly to analyze color values or object features on your screen in real time. All processing occurs entirely in volatile memory on your device.
  • No Storage: Live camera feeds, scanned items, and color samples are never saved, recorded, or transmitted to any remote server.
  • Photo Library: When you choose to inspect an existing image, it is read locally. We never upload your photos or photo metadata.

3. Tracking & Analytics

Our apps contain zero third-party software development kits (SDKs), advertising networks, or persistent user tracking mechanisms. We do not build personal profiles, sell behavioral data, or track your activity across other apps or websites.

4. Contact & Support

If you contact our support team via email, your email address and message contents are used solely to assist you with your inquiry. We do not add support inquiries to marketing mailing lists.

For privacy or support questions, reach out directly at: support@tkhtek.com
